Place the real Excel sample files here if you want the "Download Attached Sample" button to serve them.

Filenames the app will look for (in order):
- Gate_In_Out_System.xlsx
- Residents, Shops & Hotels Data.xlsx

To use:
1. Copy your Excel files into this folder with the exact filenames above, for example:
	- samples/Gate_In_Out_System.xlsx
	- samples/Residents, Shops & Hotels Data.xlsx
2. Restart your dev server (if needed) so the static file is served.

If none are present, the app will fall back to a generated demo sample when you click the button.
